A different coast from Miami Beach

Fort Lauderdale is Broward. The beach hotels are here, the Intracoastal condos are here, and Las Olas is here, where a restaurant often has an open side to the sidewalk. That open side is a zip screen conversation. A guest room facing the Atlantic is more often a blackout roller shade or drapery. Both happen in this city. They are not the same opening, and they are not priced as one package.

Waterfront condos take sun off the water, with a dock below instead of a downtown street. Impact windows are common and they do not replace a shade. Screen or solar fabric is the usual glare layer on a living-room wall. Bedrooms still ask for blackout. We measure the glass that is there, including frame depth.

Patios, and what the photos are

Las Olas and the beach strips add patios. A zip screen rides in side tracks on an outdoor frame so the cloth stays put. It is for sun, insects, and everyday breeze. It is not a storm shutter. We look at the frame before we price it. A café bay and a long hotel terrace are priced from their own measurements. The drive is motorized, specified at the visit the way an indoor motorized shade is.

There is no Fort Lauderdale project page on this site yet. Eden Roc, Faena, and Osteria Morini are Miami Beach jobs. They are linked so you can see drapery, shades, and motorized work. They are not Fort Lauderdale buildings. The Broward visit is the same kind of appointment: we come to you, measuring is free, and the estimate follows the openings.

Questions

Are the Eden Roc and Faena photos in Fort Lauderdale?

No. Those projects are on Miami Beach. They are linked so you can see the drapery and shade work. A Fort Lauderdale job is measured at the Fort Lauderdale address.

When is a zip screen the right product?

On an outdoor frame: a patio, a pergola, or an open restaurant side. A window in a condo is a roller shade or drapery, not a zip screen.

Do you come to Las Olas and the beach hotels?

Yes. We come to the property. Measuring and the estimate are free. The price depends on the openings.
