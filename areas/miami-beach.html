Three Beach projects

Miami Beach is the hotel and restaurant side of this work. Eden Roc Miami Beach has blackout curtains and roller shades. Faena Miami Beach has motorized shades. Osteria Morini, just off Lincoln Road, has green velvet drapery and sheers. The photos on this page are from those projects. They are on this island.

If the property is in Brickell, the Grove, or Coral Gables, use the Miami page instead. The products are the same list. The light and the buildings are not.

Ocean light and dining rooms

Morning sun comes off the Atlantic into east-facing guest rooms. A shade that stops short of the sill wakes the room. Blackout cloth, or blackout drapery with a roller shade behind it, is the usual guest-room answer. That pairing is what we installed at Eden Roc. Lobbies and restaurants are different. They want the water or the street in view, with the glare taken down. That is screen fabric or a sheer, not a blackout panel.

Osteria Morini is the restaurant version: velvet for the evening room, sheers so the glass still has a layer when the velvet is open. Lincoln Road and the blocks around it are full of tall dining-room glass. The cloth is chosen for that glass.

Motors, terraces, and the visit

Motorized shades, as at Faena, make sense when a bank of windows should move together or a chain would hang in a finished room. The control, a remote or an app-style system, is chosen at the measure. We do not assign a motor brand before that visit. Terraces and pool decks that need an outdoor screen, rather than a window shade, are zip screens.

Houses and condos on the Beach use the same products. We come to the building. Measuring and the estimate are free. The price depends on the openings. Call 305-951-0485 or use the estimate form. Properties farther up the coast are on the Fort Lauderdale and Boca Raton and Palm Beach pages.

Questions

Which Miami Beach projects can I look at?

Eden Roc for blackout drapery and roller shades, Faena for motorized shades, and Osteria Morini, just off Lincoln Road, for velvet and sheers.

What do east-facing guest rooms usually need?

Darkness for sleep. That is blackout fabric or blackout drapery, sometimes with a shade at the glass, as at Eden Roc.

Do you measure Beach condos, not only hotels?

Yes. We come to the building. The measure and the estimate are free, and the price depends on the openings.
