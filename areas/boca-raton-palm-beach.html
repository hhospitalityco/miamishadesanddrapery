Houses, patios, and resort dining from Boca Raton through West Palm Beach, including Eataly West Palm Beach.

Two cities, one county

Boca Raton and the Palm Beaches share a county and not a building type. Boca is lower and more residential: Mizner Park dining, club rooms, and houses whose patio can take a zip screen. West Palm Beach and Palm Beach are the downtown and resort side. Eataly West Palm Beach is a hospitality project of ours there. This site does not have Eataly photographs. The drape and shade galleries you can open, Eden Roc, Faena, and Osteria Morini, are Miami Beach. They are linked so the work is visible. They are not Palm Beach buildings.

Palm Beach island houses and West Palm condos often have one or two elevations of hard sun and a lot of quieter glass. A whole-house list is still measured opening by opening. Screen fabric where you want the garden in view. Blackout in bedrooms. Ripplefold drapery where the room should read as finished cloth rather than a bare shade. Ceiling tracks are chosen when we can see the ceiling.

Dining rooms and the Eataly project

Club and restaurant dining in Boca and West Palm sits between a beach hotel and a house. Lunch needs glare control. Dinner needs the room quieter and darker. A sheer plus a heavier cloth, as at Osteria Morini, is one way. A motorized shade is another, when the glass is the wall and the staff should move it together. Eataly West Palm Beach is the local hospitality reference for that kind of room. The product for a given opening is chosen at the measure, not from the city name.

Boca patios are the outdoor half of this page. A zip screen is for the frame outside, not for the impact window in the living room. We look at the mounting surface before we price it. A single bay and a long club terrace are different measurements.

Questions

Is Eataly West Palm Beach one of your projects?

Yes. Eataly West Palm Beach is a hospitality project of ours. This site does not have a photo gallery for it.

Are Boca Raton and West Palm Beach the same kind of job?

No. Boca is more houses, patios, and low-rise dining. West Palm and Palm Beach add downtown and resort rooms. Each opening is still measured on its own.
