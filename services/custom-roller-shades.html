What a roller shade is

A roller shade is one panel of fabric on a tube. Down, it covers the glass. Up, the opening is clear. Stock widths leave a gap or a pinch on the tall glass common in South Florida hotels, restaurants, and condos. A custom shade follows the opening you have, including the frame depth and anything that sticks into it.

Fabric options

Screen and solar cloths are woven so you can still see out while the fabric cuts glare. They suit a lobby, a dining room, or a living room where the view is part of the room. A darker screen usually handles glare more firmly. A lighter screen keeps the room brighter. During the visit we hold samples at your glass, in the light that room actually gets.

Blackout fabric is for guest rooms, bedrooms, and media rooms that need darkness. The cloth blocks light. Light can still leak at the edges if the shade does not cover the frame, so the measure includes whether the shade sits inside the opening or on the face of it. At Eden Roc Miami Beach, blackout curtains and roller shades are used together: drapery for the room, a shade for the glass.

A zebra shade alternates sheer and opaque bands. Lined up, you see through the sheer stripes. Shifted, the opaque bands cover the sheer ones. It sits between an open view and a covered window without rolling all the way up. It is not a substitute for blackout when a room has to go dark for sleep.

How the free measure works

We come to you. There is no showroom you have to travel to. We measure each opening, note cranks, mullions, and door swings, and talk through fabric and whether the shade will be manual or motorized. Measuring and the estimate are free. The price depends on those measurements, the fabric, and the hardware. A bathroom window and a restaurant bay are not the same job.

After you approve the estimate, the shades are fabricated to those sizes and we return to install. For a hotel or restaurant we coordinate with the designer or the property so rooms can stay on schedule. Patios and pergolas are a different product: outdoor zip screens. Drapery, when the room needs cloth that stays in view, is on the custom drapery page.

We measure in Miami, Miami Beach, Fort Lauderdale, and Boca Raton and Palm Beach.

Questions

What is the difference between a screen shade and a blackout shade?

Screen and solar fabrics filter glare and leave a view. Blackout fabric is for darkening a room. The choice follows how that window is used.

Will a zebra shade make a bedroom dark enough to sleep?

Closed zebra bands cover the glass, but they are not blackout. Ask for blackout fabric when the room has to go dark.

Do you measure houses as well as hotels?

Yes. We come to homes, condos, restaurants, and hotels. Measuring and the estimate are free.

How is a roller shade priced?

After the measure, from the opening size, the fabric, and the hardware. Two windows in one room can differ, so we do not publish a flat price.
