What motorized shades are

A motorized shade is a roller shade that moves without a chain. A remote, a wall switch, or a phone app sends it up or down. The idea matches the remote and app controls people already know. We do not lock an estimate to one motor brand. At the visit we match the control to the room and to how the building wants the shades to operate.

Guest rooms, long banks of restaurant glass, and finished interiors are awkward places for a chain. A motor lets a guest or a manager move one shade or a group. Faena Miami Beach is a motorized shade project: the shades are part of the room rather than a cord in the view.

Controls and fabric

A handheld remote is the simple version. A wall switch stays with the room. App-style control helps when someone wants shades grouped or on a schedule. We confirm which of those you need while we are there. Power is part of the same conversation. Some openings can take a battery motor. Others are better on a wire. We look before recommending either, and we do not promise a battery life before the measure.

The motor is the drive. The fabric is still the light control: screen, solar, blackout, or zebra. Motorizing a shade does not change which cloth is right for sleep versus a view. Outdoor tracked screens are separate and are covered on the zip screen page.

How the free measure works

We come to the property. We measure the glass, note power nearby, and talk through who will run the shades. Measuring and the estimate are free. The price depends on the openings, the fabric, and the control. A single bedroom and a ballroom wall are scoped separately.

We install in hotels, restaurants, and homes across Miami Beach, Miami, Fort Lauderdale, and Boca Raton and Palm Beach. If the room needs cloth that stays in view rather than a shade in a tube, see custom drapery.

Questions

Do you require a particular motor brand?

No. At the measure we specify the control the room needs: a remote, a wall switch, or app-style operation. A brand is not promised in advance.

Can several shades move together?

Yes, when they are grouped. That is common on a restaurant front or a row of guest-room windows. Grouping is decided on site.

Will a motorized shade work in a condo?

Often, yes. The limit is the opening and the power, which we check when we visit. We come to the unit.

How is the price set?

From the measurements, the fabric, and the control, after a free measure. We do not publish a flat motorized-shade price.
