Tanda Tango & Social Club

Custom ceiling & wall drapery.

In a venue where music, movement, and mood are everything, The Hospitality House was commissioned to create a transformative ceiling and wall drapery concept that echoes the graceful energy of Argentine tango. Tanda Tango & Social Club is not just a dance venue—it’s an experience. Our work was designed to reflect that soul.

‍

‍

‍

Our Work

‍

The centerpiece of this project is a breathtaking custom ceiling installation composed of hundreds of small, hand-formed ripple panels. These sculptural fabric forms flow outward from the chandelier in rhythmic waves, mimicking the swirling motion of dancers mid-step. Light was intentionally layered between the folds to cast a soft, dynamic glow—bringing the ceiling to life with every beat of music and movement below.

‍

Throughout the club, we installed rich, navy velvet drapery, adding drama, depth, and acoustic softness. These floor-to-ceiling panels not only frame the architectural arches with elegance but also ground the ethereal ceiling installation with warmth and contrast.

‍

‍

Impact

‍

The result is a truly immersive interior environment where design and movement converge. Our drapery installation elevates the entire space into an elegant and evocative celebration of dance, capturing the emotion and artistry that define the Tanda experience.
