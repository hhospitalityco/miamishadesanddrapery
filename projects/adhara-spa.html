Adhara Medical Spa

Custom black out drapery.

At Adhara Medical Spa, traquility and privacy are essential to the client experience. The Hospitality House was selected to craft a drapery solution that would complement the spa's serene design and enhance both function and form throughout the space.

‍

‍

Our Work

‍

We designed and installed fully custom blackout drapery in a warm, neutral tone to bring softness and intimacy to the spa's modern interior. The drapery was strategically placed to divide open areas, create private treatment zones, and reduce ambient light and sound - offering guests a peaceful, cocooned experience. Our floor-to-ceiling application, paired with the soft architectural lighting and natural wood finishes, added visual warmth while subtly guiding the flow of the space.

‍

‍

Impact

‍

The drapery transformed the spa's corridors and communal areas into calming, private sanctuaries. By blending functionality with aesthetics, our work helped reinforce Adhara's brand of quiet luxury and personalized care.
