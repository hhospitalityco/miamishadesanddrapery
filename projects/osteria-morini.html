Osteria Morini

Custom drapery.

Nested just off Lincoln Road, Osteria Morini brings a refined Italian dining experience to the heart of Miami Beach. The Hospitality House was selected to elevate the interiors with custom window treatments that would reflect the restaurant's sophisticated and welcoming ambiance.

‍

‍

Our Work

‍

We designed and installed luxurious custom green velvet drapery paired with light green sheer panels, creating a layered look that softens the space while enhancing its architectural lines. The velvet adds richness and depth, while the sheets gently diffuse natural light - creating an inviting, elegant atmosphere from day to night. This design not only complements the restaurant's European charm but also draws the eye upward, emphasizing the space's dramatic ceiling height.

‍

Impact

‍

Our tailored drapery solution brought visual cohesion and warmth to the space, enhancing the guest experience and elevating the restaurant's overall design. The combination of texture, color, and scale perfectly aligns with Osteria Morini's upscale yet approachable brand.
